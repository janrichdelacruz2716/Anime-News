Sakamoto Days Cour 2 Release Date Confirmed

Sakamoto Days Cour 2 is set to premiere in July 2025. The exact date has not been announced, but it will air on TV Tokyo and affiliated networks in Japan, with Netflix streaming the series worldwid. Sakamoto Days' success was no surprise. With its hilarious premise, sharp action, and endearing characters, the anime adaptation of Yuto Suzuki's hit manga became an instant sensation. The first cour of season one wrapped up in style, leaving fans eagerly awaiting the next installment. Thankfully, the wait won’t be long, as Cour 2 is set to premiere soon. Adding to the excitement, a brand-new visual was recently unveiled at AnimeJapan 2025, teasing what’s in store for viewers.

Sakamoto Days follows Taro Sakamoto, a once-legendary hitman who left his violent past behind for a peaceful life as a family man and convenience store owner. However, trouble often comes knocking, forcing Sakamoto to channel his dormant combat skills while protecting his family and friends. The comedic yet action-packed narrative has struck a chord with audiences worldwide. The anime’s explosive success speaks for itself. Sakamoto Days ranked #2 globally for non-English shows on Netflix, amassing 8.6 million views within its debut week, making it Netflix's biggest anime premiere ever. It also topped the charts in multiple countries and even broke into the U.S. Top 10. With Cour 1’s popularity, expectations are sky-high for the second half of the season.
