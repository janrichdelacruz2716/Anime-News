Kaiju No 8 Season 2 Release Date Confirmed

Kaiju No. 8 Season 2 is set to release in July 2025. A teaser visual and trailer were released on March 22, 2025, providing fans with a glimpse of what to expect from the upcoming season. Dandandan S2 Release Date Confirmed

Dandadan's second season is set to premiere on July 3, 2025, in Japan.
