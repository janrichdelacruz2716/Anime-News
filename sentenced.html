Sentenced to Be a Hero Anime Release Date Confirmed

The anime adaptation of "Sentenced to Be a Hero" is set to premiere in October 2025. Studio KAI is producing the series, and the main characters are voiced by Yōhei Azakami as Xylo Forbartz and Mayu Iizuka as Teoritta. The anime was first teased with a trailer in March 2024, and more details, including a new key visual, were revealed in March 2025

Sentenced to Be a Hero Anime Release Date Confirmed

The upcoming TV anime adaptation of Rocket Shokai and MEPHISTO's Sentenced to Be a Hero light novels today revealed an epic teaser visual, a 2025 release date and two main cast members. Studio KAI (Skeleton Knight in Another World) is producing the series' animation. Key staff members, including the director, are yet to be confirmed at the time of reporting.
