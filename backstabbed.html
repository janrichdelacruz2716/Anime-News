Backstabbed in a Backwater Dungeon Anime Release Date Confirmed

The anime adaptation of "Backstabbed in a Backwater Dungeon" is set to premiere in the Fall 2025 season. The official website and accounts for the anime released a teaser trailer and visual on April 17, 2025, confirming the Fall 2025 broadcast and the series' main cast and staff. The anime is produced by J.C.Staff, directed by Katsushi Sakurabi, with scripts written by Hiroshi Ōnogi, characters designed by Yukie Suzuki, and music composed by Ryo Takahashi.
