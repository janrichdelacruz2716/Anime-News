Kaiju No 8 Season 2 Release Date Confirmed

Kaiju No. 8 Season 2 is set to release in July 2025. A teaser visual and trailer were released on March 22, 2025, providing fans with a glimpse of what to expect from the upcoming season. Prior to the start of Season 2, a compilation film of scenes from the first season, paired with a special episode titled "Hoshina's Day Off," will be shown in theaters starting March 28, 2025. The one and only seventh captain of the Defense Force's First Division, Gen Narumi, made his debut in a brand-new Kaiju No. 8 Season 2 teaser trailer and set of visuals revealed at AnimeJapan 2025 today. The new season will stream on Crunchyroll when it premieres in July 2025.

Kaiju No 8 OVA Hoshina Day Off Release Date Confirmed

TOHO Animation has provided new updates on Season 2 of Kaiju No.8, confirming that the next installment of the highly-anticipated anime will officially premiere in 2025, summertime. A new trailer for Kaiju No. 8’s Season 1 compilation film andHoshina’s Day Off - an upcoming special episode was revealed alongside a new teaser visual at the Jump Fest 2025 event over the weekend.The brand-new teaser visual spotlights Gen Narumi, the First Division Captain of the Defense Force, with the tagline “Proof of being the strongest” unveiled, building anticipation for the upcoming season. Theaters in Japan will host a three-week run for the anime’s Season 1 compilation film as well as the special episode starting on March 28, 2025. In addition to that, the new trailer also previews “Invincible,” the anime’s new theme song by OneRepublic.Kaiju No.8 Season 2 is confirmed to release in July 2025.
