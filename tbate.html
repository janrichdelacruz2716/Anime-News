TBATE Animation Criticism

The criticism intensified following the release of the second episode, with fans expressing profound disappointment over the lack of fluid character movement, overuse of still frames, and reliance on panning shots. A petition demanding the cancellation and redo of the anime adaptation has gathered over 40,000 signatures, highlighting the extent of fan dissatisfaction. The petitioners argue that the current adaptation tarnishes the legacy of TBATE and call for a more capable studio to handle the task.

TBATE Animation Criticism

In response to the mounting criticism, the original creator of TBATE, TurtleMe, addressed the fan backlash directly in a post on Reddit. TurtleMe acknowledged the challenges faced in the anime industry and emphasized that the creator often has limited control over the technical aspects of the adaptation. He urged fans to remain respectful and supportive, drawing parallels to the early days of TBATE when it faced criticism before growing and improving over time. Despite the widespread disappointment, TurtleMe and other supporters of the series maintain that the story and artwork of TBATE have potential, and they hope that the animation quality will improve in future episodes.
