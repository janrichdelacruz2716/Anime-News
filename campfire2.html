Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill Season 2 Release Date Confirmed

Campfire Cooking in Another World with My Absurd Skill Season 2 is set to premiere in October 2025. The official X account of the anime announced the release date on May 3, 2025, confirming the second season will release in October of the same year.
