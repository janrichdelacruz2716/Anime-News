Dandandan S2 Release Date Confirmed

Dandadan's second season is set to premiere on July 3, 2025, in Japan. The first three episodes of the second season will be released as a compilation film titled DAN DA DAN: EVIL EYE, which will premiere in theaters outside of Japan. In Asia, the film will be released on May 30, 2025, and in Europe, it will be released on June 7, 2025.
