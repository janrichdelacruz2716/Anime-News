To Your Eternity Season 3 Release Date

After the second season of the anime adaptation of Yoshitoki Ōima's To Your Eternity completed its run in March 2023, the third season was announced, but not much information about the series has come out until now. The series' official X (formerly known as Twitter) account confirmed on Thursday that To Your Eternity season 3 is set to broadcast in October, forming part of the Fall 2025 anime season.

To Your Eternity Season 3 Release Date

The third season of To Your Eternity was originally announced in March 2023, following the end of the second season. The upcoming season will be adapting the "This World" arc of the manga (also referred to as the "New World" arc), which will feature Fushi's journey as it continues into the modern era and brings him face-to-face with the challenges of our technologically advanced society.
